Payment records

Keep a person-by-person record of what you collect and what you pay, with payment modes, monthly dues and printable statements.

Funds and team

Run shared funds with contributors, track employee attendance, and log salary and advance payments in one place.

Invoices

Build bills from your price list, add GST and discounts, accept cash, UPI, card or credit, and print an A4 invoice or an 80 mm receipt.
