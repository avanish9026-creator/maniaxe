Sit up straight

Feet flat on the floor, wrists floating just above the keyboard, screen at eye level.

Don't look down

Every glance at the keyboard costs you a fraction of a second. Trust your fingers.

Accuracy first

Speed follows accuracy, not the other way round. Slow down until mistakes disappear.
