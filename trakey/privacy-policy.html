In this policy, "Trakey", "we", "us" and "our" mean Trakey Labs and Maniaxe.in together.

This policy explains what information the App handles, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. By tapping Get started on the first screen, or by installing, opening or using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2. Summary in plain words

  • Most of what you create stays on your device.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. It is needed only for cloud backup, restore and the feedback features.
  • We do not sell your personal information or show ads.

3. Information stored on your device

These items are created by you and stored in the App's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us unless you sign in and back up, or use a feature described in section 7.

  • Profile: name, bio, avatar choice and profile photo (a photo from your gallery stays on your device).
  • Notes, goals and milestones, tasks, habits and habit history, calendar entries, reminders and alerts, and the Organise tab.
  • Focus sessions and activity statistics, game and challenge statistics.
  • Learn cards, your custom question folders, imported questions, Challenge history and analytics.
  • Calculator history, saved currency choices and rate alerts, world clock cities, saved Countries items.
  • Translator history and saved translations.
  • Voice recordings you make with the Recorder, saved as audio files on your device.
  • Images you choose to save from Challenge results, which are written to the Trakey folder in your device's Pictures.
  • Music player library and playlists. The player reads audio files you choose or that your device exposes to it, and plays them on your device.
  • App settings such as theme, notification choices, Trakey AI settings and your own Gemini API key, if you enter one.
  • App lock settings, if you turn on App lock (see section 11).

You can use these features without creating an account.

4. Information from your Google account

If you choose Continue with Google, Trakey receives your name, email address, a unique account ID and a link to your profile photo through Firebase Authentication, a service of Google. We use them to show your profile, to identify your backup, to keep your account index entry (section 5) and to let you send feedback. We do not receive your Google password.

5. Cloud backup, Trakey Web sync and your account entry

If you are signed in, Trakey can back up your data to your account automatically about every 3 hours and when you leave the App, and you can use Back up now at any time. Restores happen only when you choose Restore or accept the restore offer after signing in.

A backup can include your profile details (name, email, bio and location if you filled them in), notes, goals, habits, tasks, Learn and Challenge content and statistics, game statistics, settings and your Gemini API key. It never includes your profile photo, music, recordings or temporary history (calculator, currency and focus history). It does not include your App lock PIN or settings.

Backups are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account ID, compressed. Google protects data in transit and at rest. The App does not add a separate encryption layer of its own, so do not store anything in Trakey that you would not want held in a cloud database.

While you are signed in, your notes, goals, tasks, question folders and the Learn cards you add are also kept in sync with Trakey Web (maniaxe.in/trakey), so an edit made on the phone appears on the web and the other way round. This happens continuously, not only when you back up.

When you back up, the App also saves an account entry in our admin database: your account ID, name, email, bio, location, the date you joined, when you were last active and your app version. We use it to run the service, answer support requests and understand how many people use the App. Access is limited to the Trakey team. Your rating and feedback status is saved to your account automatically.

6. Trakey AI, translation and voice

Trakey AI, the translator, image translation, Learn scan (photos, PDF pages or text files you pick to turn into cards) and question import recovery work through Trakey's AI server, which runs on Cloudflare. When you use these features, the text, images, documents or voice-transcribed text you submit, together with the instructions needed for the feature, are sent to that server and forwarded to an AI provider to generate a response: Groq for most text requests, and Google (Gemini API) for photos, documents, scans and as a backup when the first provider is busy. These providers process your content under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit anything you do not want to be processed by them.

To recognise the App and apply daily limits, the server checks a sign-in token. If you are not signed in, the App automatically creates an anonymous guest account in Firebase Authentication, with no name or email. The server keeps daily request counters linked to your account ID and, for guest accounts, a short one-way fingerprint of your network address to prevent abuse. Trakey's server does not store the content of your AI requests or the answers. Cloudflare, Groq and Google may process technical request data such as your IP address under their own policies.

You can add your own Gemini API key in Settings > Customise. If you do, your Trakey AI requests are sent directly from your device to Google with your key and do not go through our server. Your own key is stored on your device and, if you back up, in your backup. Anyone with access to your key can use your quota, so keep it private.

Voice input uses the speech recognition service of your device or Google, and text-to-speech uses your device's speech engine. Their handling of audio is governed by the provider of that service.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. See the Terms and conditions.

7. Third-party services the App connects to

To work, the App connects to the services below. Each service receives at least your IP address and technical request data, as with any internet request.

  • Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging: sign-in, backup, sync, announcements and update notes, and push notifications.
  • Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database): runs Trakey's AI server, which checks your sign-in token, applies daily limits and forwards AI requests (section 6).
  • Groq: writes the answers for most Trakey AI, alert and task, and translation text requests (section 6).
  • Google Gemini API: photos, documents, Learn scan, question import and backup for the services above (section 6).
  • Google speech recognition and text-to-speech: voice features.
  • Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com): delivers your feedback and support messages to us by email.
  • Microsoft Clarity: we use it for app growth, to understand how the App is used.
  • Currency rate providers (Frankfurter, the jsDelivr-hosted currency API and open.er-api.com): the App requests public exchange rates for the currency codes you view. No personal data is sent beyond what is needed to make the request.
  • GitHub: the App checks the latest release to tell you about updates, and downloads the update file only if you ask.
  • Google profile photo servers: to download your Google profile photo once and keep a small copy in the App's cache.
  • Links you open (Telegram, YouTube, our website, Google Maps and Google AI Studio) open in your browser or another app, where that service's own policy applies.

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

8. Permissions

Each permission is optional, is used only for the feature named, and can be changed at any time in your system settings.

  • Notifications: reminders, alerts, rate alerts and announcements.
  • Exact alarms: so that reminders fire at the exact time you set.
  • Microphone: voice input, Translator voice mode and the Recorder.
  • Camera and photos or files: image translation, Learn scan, profile photo, saving images, and importing questions or music that you pick.
  • Internet and network state: features in section 7.
  • Biometrics (optional): to unlock App lock with your fingerprint or face. The App never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data. Your device handles it and only tells the App whether the check passed.

Trakey does not request your contacts, SMS, call log or precise location.

9. Push notifications and announcements

Your device is subscribed to a Trakey announcement topic through Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send app news, updates and notices. Google creates an FCM token for your installation for this purpose. You can turn notifications off in the App or in system settings. The App also reads announcements and update notes that we publish, and checks whether your installed version is still supported. If it is not, the App asks you to update before you continue.

10. Feedback, ratings and support

When you send feedback, a rating or a message, Trakey sends what you write, your name, your email address if available, the date and time, your rating, the app version and your device make, model and Android version to Trakey support through Web3Forms. A copy is also stored in our admin database together with your account ID, so we can find and answer it. We use it only to respond, fix problems and improve the App, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes.

11. App lock and security

App lock is optional. You turn it on in Settings by creating a PIN of 4 to 6 digits. If you turn it on:

  • Trakey locks again after you have been away from the App for 2 minutes. If Extra secure is on, it locks after 1 minute.
  • Extra secure hides Trakey's preview in your phone's recent apps list and blocks screenshots and screen recording inside Trakey. It is off until you turn it on.
  • Your PIN is never stored as plain text. Only a salted, hashed form of it is stored in the App's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up by Trakey or shared.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock, if you enable it, is handled entirely by Android.
  • After several wrong PIN attempts the App makes you wait before trying again.
  • Your PIN cannot be recovered by us. If you forget it, tap Forgot PIN on the lock screen and confirm with your phone's own screen lock to reset it. If your phone has no screen lock, the only way to reset it is to clear the App's data in Android settings, which erases local data that has not been backed up.
  • To change your PIN or turn App lock off, you must enter your current PIN.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: data in the App's private storage is protected by Android's app sandbox, and connections to our services use encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock. If the device is rooted or compromised, local data may be exposed.

16. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it, clear it from Account > Backup & data, or uninstall the App.
  • Cloud backups stay until you delete them from the App or ask us to.

Feedback and support messages you already sent stay in our records until you ask us to delete them.
  • Signing out removes the signed-in account's data from the device and keeps your cloud backup for your next sign-in.
  • Feedback and support emails are kept as long as needed to help you and improve the App.
  • To ask for deletion of anything else we hold about you, email support.trakey@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time and in any case within the time the law requires. Some records may be kept where the law requires or for legitimate security reasons.

21. Friends and challenges

Friends is optional and needs a Google sign-in.

  • Username: you choose a username of 3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores. Anyone who knows it exactly can find you, see your name, photo or avatar and send you a friend request. Your username, display name, avatar number and Google photo link are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and are visible to people who search for your username and to your friends. If you have not chosen a built-in avatar, your Google profile photo (its link is stored with your Friends profile) is also shown to them; if you choose an avatar, that is shown instead. Photos from your gallery, your email address, notes, goals, tasks and your own questions are never shown to other users.
  • Friend requests and friendships are stored until the request is accepted, declined or cancelled, or the friend is removed.
  • Share my stats (Settings > Friends) is off by default. When it is on, your EXP, challenges taken and accuracy are stored so your friends can see them in their friend view and leaderboard. Turning it off deletes that card.
  • Challenges: when you challenge a friend, the title, level and questions (taken from Trakey's built-in sets only) and each player's score, time and finish status are stored for both of you for about 7 days.
  • You can change your username, remove a friend, cancel a request or turn sharing off at any time. To ask us to delete your username, profile and Friends data, email support.trakey@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.
