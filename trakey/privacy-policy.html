1. Who we are

Trakey (the “App”) is a local-first productivity, learning and personal growth app for Android. It is developed and managed by Trakey Labs, which is owned by Rohan, and is powered by Maniaxe.in, which is owned by Avanish. In this policy, “Trakey”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Trakey Labs and Maniaxe.in together.

This policy explains what information the App handles, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. By tapping Get started on the first screen, or by installing, opening or using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2. Summary in plain words

  • Most of what you create stays on your device.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. It is needed only for cloud backup, restore, Friends, feedback and Trakey Pro.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Trakey does not show banner or pop-up ads.
  • Some features send data to third-party services (Google Firebase, Cloudflare, Groq, Google Gemini, Google AdMob, Web3Forms, Microsoft Clarity, a payment provider when you pay, and public rate or update servers). Section 7 lists exactly which.
  • You can clear your local data and delete your cloud backup from the App at any time.
  • The App is not meant for children under 13.
  • A feature guide explains gestures and features that are easy to miss (section 23).

3. Information stored on your device

These items are created by you and stored in the App’s private storage on your device. They are not sent to us unless you sign in and back up, or use a feature described in section 7.

  • Profile: name, bio, avatar choice and profile photo (a photo from your gallery stays on your device).
  • Notes, goals and milestones, tasks, habits and habit history, calendar entries and countdowns, reminders and alerts, checklists, expenses and budgets, and the Organise tab.
  • Focus sessions and activity statistics, weekly and monthly report data, game and challenge statistics.
  • Learn cards and learning progress, spaced-review data, your custom question folders, imported questions, Challenge history and analytics.
  • Calculator history, saved currency choices and rate alerts, world clock cities, saved Countries items.
  • Translator history and saved translations.
  • Voice recordings you make with the Recorder, saved as audio files on your device.
  • Images you choose to save from Challenge results, notes, expenses or reports, which are written to the Trakey folder in your device’s Pictures.
  • Scan: pages you add to Scan are held in memory only while Scan is open. Photos or PDFs you share to Trakey from another app are copied into the App’s private cache so they can be read, and are deleted automatically after about a day. A PDF you make with Save as PDF is saved in the Trakey folder inside your device’s Downloads (Android 10 and later) or handed to the app you share it with. Text you extract or summarise is kept only if you copy it, share it or save it as a Note.
  • Music player library and playlists. The player reads audio files you choose or that your device exposes to it, and plays them on your device.
  • App settings such as theme, language, text size, notification choices, the default settings for new alerts, your Quick access tools, Trakey AI settings and your own Gemini API key, if you enter one.
  • Your region: Trakey checks, on your device, the SIM and network country, the phone’s region setting or its time zone, to decide, for example, which question sets to show and whether reminders are written in Hinglish. This needs no permission and is not sent anywhere.
  • App lock settings, if you turn on App lock (see section 11).

You can use these features without creating an account.

4. Information from your Google account

If you choose Continue with Google, Trakey receives your name, email address, a unique account ID and a link to your profile photo through Firebase Authentication, a service of Google. We use them to show your profile, to identify your backup, to keep your account index entry (section 5), to let you send feedback and to link Trakey Pro to your account. We do not receive your Google password.

5. Cloud backup, Trakey Web sync and your account entry

If you are signed in, Trakey can back up your data to your account automatically about every 3 hours and when you leave the App, and you can use Back up now at any time. Restores happen only when you choose Restore or accept the restore offer after signing in.

A backup can include your profile details (name, email, bio and location if you filled them in), notes, goals, habits, tasks, calendar, checklists, expenses, Learn and Challenge content and statistics, game statistics, settings and your Gemini API key. It never includes your profile photo, music, recordings or temporary history (calculator, currency and focus history). It does not include your App lock PIN or settings.

If you allow it, backups are stored in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only Trakey can open and that uses your Drive storage (Trakey requests only the Google Drive app-data permission and cannot see your other Drive files). Your most important data (profile, settings, notes, goals, tasks, habits, calendar, alerts and achievements) and a small backup information record are always stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account ID, compressed, so they can be restored even without Google Drive. Google protects data in transit and at rest. The App does not add a separate encryption layer of its own, so do not store anything in Trakey that you would not want held in a cloud database.

While you are signed in, your notes, goals, tasks, habits, calendar events, alerts, question folders and the Learn cards you add are also kept in sync across your devices (and, for some of them, with Trakey Web at maniaxe.in/trakey), so an edit made on one device appears on the others. This happens continuously, not only when you back up. You can turn it off in Account, Backup & data.

When you back up, the App also saves an account entry in our admin database: your account ID, name, email, bio, location, the date you joined, when you were last active and your app version. We use it to run the service, answer support requests and understand how many people use the App. Access is limited to the Trakey team. Your rating and feedback status is saved to your account automatically.

Separately from Trakey’s own backup, Android’s device backup (Google backup and device-to-device transfer) may copy some of the App’s data. Trakey excludes the App lock PIN, recordings and some temporary data from it.

6. Trakey AI, translation and voice

Trakey AI, the translator, image translation, Learn scan and Scan with AI (photos, PDF pages or text files you pick to turn into cards, text or summaries) and question import recovery work through Trakey’s AI server, which runs on Cloudflare. When you use these features, the text, images, documents or voice-transcribed text you submit, together with the instructions needed for the feature, are sent to that server and forwarded to an AI provider to generate a response: Groq for most text requests, and Google (Gemini API) for photos, documents, scans and as a backup when the first provider is busy. These providers process your content under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit anything you do not want to be processed by them.

Some Scan tools run only on your device and send nothing: Save as PDF, Extract text with the On phone option (which uses Google ML Kit text recognition on your device), and copying the text of PDFs that already contain text. Choosing AI in Extract text, or using Summary, sends your pages, or the text read from your PDF, as described above.

To recognise the App and apply daily limits, the server checks a sign-in token. If you are not signed in, the App automatically creates an anonymous guest account in Firebase Authentication, with no name or email. The server keeps daily request counters linked to your account ID and, for guest accounts, a short one-way fingerprint of your network address to prevent abuse. Trakey’s server does not store the content of your AI requests or the answers. Cloudflare, Groq and Google may process technical request data such as your IP address under their own policies.

You can add your own Gemini API key in Settings > Customise. If you do, your Trakey AI requests are sent directly from your device to Google with your key and do not go through our server. Your own key is stored on your device and, if you back up, in your backup. Anyone with access to your key can use your quota, so keep it private.

Voice input (including holding the AI button to speak) uses the speech recognition service of your device or Google, and text-to-speech uses your device’s speech engine. Their handling of audio is governed by the provider of that service.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. See the Terms and Conditions.

7. Third-party services the App connects to

To work, the App connects to the services below. Each service receives at least your IP address and technical request data, as with any internet request.

  • Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging: sign-in, backup, sync, Friends, Trakey Pro records, announcements and update notes, and push notifications.
  • Google Drive (app-data folder): backups, only if you allow it (section 5).
  • Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database): runs Trakey’s AI server, which checks your sign-in token, applies daily limits and forwards AI requests (section 6), and the payment server used when you pay through a payment provider (section 22).
  • Groq: writes the answers for most Trakey AI, alert and task, and translation text requests (section 6).
  • Google Gemini API: photos, documents, Learn scan, Scan (Extract text with AI and Summary), question import and backup for the services above (section 6).
  • Google ML Kit text recognition: runs on your device to read text from photos when you choose Extract text > On phone. Your photos are not sent anywhere for this.
  • Google speech recognition and text-to-speech: voice features.
  • Google AdMob: its software library is part of the App. It may be contacted when you reach a free daily limit (section 22).
  • PayU: a payment gateway that may be offered when you buy Trakey Pro (section 22).
  • Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com): delivers your feedback and support messages to us by email.
  • Microsoft Clarity: we use it for app growth, to understand how the App is used, such as which screens are opened and where people tap.
  • Currency rate providers (Frankfurter, the jsDelivr-hosted currency API and open.er-api.com): the App requests public exchange rates for the currency codes you view. No personal data is sent beyond what is needed to make the request.
  • GitHub: the App checks the latest release to tell you about updates, and downloads the update file only if you ask.
  • Google profile photo servers: to download your Google profile photo once and keep a small copy in the App’s cache.
  • Links you open (Telegram, YouTube, WhatsApp, our website, Google Maps and Google AI Studio) open in your browser or another app, where that service’s own policy applies. When you share a note or file to another app, that app receives what you chose to share.

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy

8. Permissions

Each permission is optional, is used only for the feature named, and can be changed at any time in your system settings.

  • Notifications: reminders, alerts, rate alerts and announcements.
  • Exact alarms: so that reminders and alerts fire at the exact time you set.
  • Vibrate, wake lock, full-screen notification, foreground service and start after reboot: so that an Alert can ring like an alarm, show over the lock screen, play its sound, and be scheduled again after your phone restarts or the App updates.
  • Microphone: voice input, Translator voice mode and the Recorder.
  • Camera and photos or files: image translation, Learn scan, Scan, profile photo, saving images, and importing questions or music that you pick.
  • Share menu: when you share photos or PDFs to Trakey, Android gives Trakey access only to the files you shared.
  • Internet and network state: features in section 7.
  • Biometrics (optional): to unlock App lock with your fingerprint or face. The App never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data. Your device handles it and only tells the App whether the check passed.
  • Floating timer (picture-in-picture), Home screen shortcuts and the Today widget use standard Android features and need no extra permission. The widget shows your upcoming alerts and tasks on your home screen, and is drawn on your device.

Trakey does not request your contacts, SMS, call log or precise location.

9. Push notifications and announcements

Your device is subscribed to a Trakey announcement topic through Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can send app news, updates and notices. Google creates an FCM token for your installation for this purpose. You can turn notifications off in the App or in system settings. The App also reads announcements and update notes that we publish, and checks whether your installed version is still supported. If it is not, the App asks you to update before you continue.

10. Feedback, ratings and support

When you send feedback, a rating or a message, Trakey sends what you write, your name, your email address if available, the date and time, your rating, the app version and your device make, model and Android version to Trakey support through Web3Forms. A copy is also stored in our admin database together with your account ID, so we can find and answer it. 11. App lock and security

App lock is optional. You turn it on in Settings by creating a PIN of 4 to 6 digits. If you turn it on:

  • Trakey locks again after you have been away from the App for 2 minutes. If Extra secure is on, it locks after 1 minute.
  • Extra secure hides Trakey’s preview in your phone’s recent apps list and blocks screenshots and screen recording inside Trakey. It is off until you turn it on.
  • Your PIN is never stored as plain text. Only a salted, hashed form of it is stored in the App’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up by Trakey or shared.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock, if you enable it, is handled entirely by Android.
  • After several wrong PIN attempts the App makes you wait before trying again.
  • Your PIN cannot be recovered by us. If you forget it, tap Forgot PIN on the lock screen and confirm with your phone’s own screen lock to reset it. If your phone has no screen lock, the only way to reset it is to clear the App’s data in Android settings, which erases local data that has not been backed up.
  • To change your PIN or turn App lock off, you must enter your current PIN.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures: data in the App’s private storage is protected by Android’s app sandbox, and connections to our services use encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock. If the device is rooted or compromised, local data may be exposed.

16. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it, clear it from Account > Backup & data, or uninstall the App.
  • Photos and PDFs shared to Trakey are copied to the App’s cache and removed automatically after about a day. Files you save yourself, such as a PDF in Downloads, stay until you delete them.
  • Cloud backups stay until you delete them from the App or ask us to.
  • Delete all data in Account > Backup & data removes your cloud backup, the synced copies of notes, goals, tasks, questions and Learn cards, your rating status and your account entry, and clears local data on the device. Feedback and support messages you already sent stay in our records until you ask us to delete them.
  • Payment records (section 22) may be kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes, even after you delete your other data.
  • Signing out removes the signed-in account’s data from the device and keeps your cloud backup for your next sign-in.
  • Feedback and support emails are kept as long as needed to help you and improve the App.
  • Analytics, advertising and provider logs are kept under the relevant provider’s own settings and retention policy.
  • To ask for deletion of anything else we hold about you, email support.trakey@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time and in any case within the time the law requires. Some records may be kept where the law requires or for legitimate security reasons.

21. Friends and challenges

Friends is optional and needs a Google sign-in.

  • Username: you choose a username of 3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores. Anyone who knows it exactly can find you, see your name, photo or avatar and send you a friend request. Your username, display name, avatar number and Google photo link are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and are visible to people who search for your username and to your friends. If you have not chosen a built-in avatar, your Google profile photo (its link is stored with your Friends profile) is also shown to them; if you choose an avatar, that is shown instead. Photos from your gallery, your email address, notes, goals, tasks and your own questions are never shown to other users.
  • Friend requests and friendships are stored until the request is accepted, declined or cancelled, or the friend is removed.
  • Share my stats (Settings > Friends) is off by default. When it is on, your EXP, challenges taken and accuracy are stored so your friends can see them in their friend view and leaderboard. Turning it off deletes that card.
  • Challenges: when you challenge a friend, the title, level and questions (taken from Trakey’s built-in sets only) and each player’s score, time and finish status are stored for both of you for about 7 days.
  • You can change your username, remove a friend, cancel a request or turn sharing off at any time. To ask us to delete your username, profile and Friends data, email support.trakey@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

22. Trakey Pro, payments and advertising software

Trakey Pro. Trakey Pro is optional. Your Pro status (free trial, free month, plan and expiry date) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID, so that a trial cannot be repeated by reinstalling the App and so that Pro follows your account. Free accounts have daily limits on some AI features; the Free vs Pro screen in the App shows the current limits.

Paying for Pro. Depending on your region and the App version, you can pay by UPI (QR code or UPI ID) or through a payment gateway such as PayU.

  • UPI: after you pay in your own payment app, you enter the UTR (transaction reference) and may attach a screenshot of the payment. We store your account ID, name, email, plan, amount, UTR, screenshot, status, time and app version so that we can verify the payment and activate Pro. A screenshot may show details from your payment app, so crop or hide anything you do not want us to see. A UTR can be used for one order only.
  • Payment gateway: the payment page is provided by PayU. Trakey’s payment server (on Cloudflare) prepares the signed payment request and checks the result with PayU, then records the order and your Pro access. PayU collects and processes your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. Trakey does not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN, bank password or OTP.

Never send us a UPI PIN, card details, password or OTP. Payment records are kept as described in section 16.

Advertising software. The App contains Google AdMob software. Trakey does not currently run advertising, but when you reach a free daily limit the App may contact Google AdMob, which may process your IP address, device and advertising identifiers and diagnostics under Google’s own policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings. We do not send your notes, goals, tasks, recordings or other content to AdMob. If rewarded ads are offered in a future version, this policy will be updated first.
