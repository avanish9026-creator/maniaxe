1. Who we are

Trakey (the "App") is a local-first productivity, learning and personal growth app for Android, developed and operated by Maniaxe.in. In this policy, "Trakey", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the operator responsible for Trakey through Maniaxe.in.

This policy explains what information the App handles, why it is processed, which services may receive it, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you. Where consent is required, we will request it through the App or another appropriate method. Please do not use features you do not agree to use under this policy.

2. Summary in plain words

  • Most information you create stays in the App's private storage on your device unless you use a feature that sends it elsewhere.
  • Google sign-in is optional for ordinary local features. It is needed for certain cloud, sync, social and feedback features.
  • Trakey offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Watching a rewarded ad can unlock access to two designated features.
  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising-related information when ads are requested, displayed or interacted with.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Some features connect to Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Cloudflare, Groq, Google Gemini, Web3Forms, Microsoft Clarity and other services described below.
  • You can clear local data and delete cloud backup data through the App where the relevant controls are available.
  • Trakey is not directed to children under 13.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information is generally stored in the App's private storage on your device. It is not automatically sent to us merely because you create it, except when you use a feature described in this policy.

  • Profile details, including your name, bio, avatar choice and selected profile photo.
  • Notes, goals, milestones, tasks, habits, habit history, calendar entries, reminders and Organise tab content.
  • Focus sessions, activity statistics, game statistics and challenge statistics.
  • Learn cards, custom question folders, imported questions, challenge history and related analytics.
  • Calculator history, saved currency choices, rate alerts, world clock cities and saved Countries items.
  • Translator history and saved translations.
  • Voice recordings created using the Recorder, stored as audio files on your device.
  • Images you choose to save from Challenge results, written to the Trakey folder in your device's Pictures directory.
  • Music player library information and playlists. The player reads audio files you choose or that your device exposes to it.
  • App settings, including theme, notification preferences, Trakey AI settings and a Gemini API key if you provide one.
  • App lock settings, where enabled.

Gallery photos, recordings, music and other files remain on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that shares or uploads them. Some features, including AI processing, cloud backup, sync and rewarded ads, involve additional processing described below.

4. Information from your Google account

If you choose Continue with Google, Trakey receives your name, email address, unique account ID and a link to your profile photo through Firebase Authentication. We use these details to display your profile, identify your backup, maintain your account entry and provide features that require sign-in. We do not receive your Google password.

5. Cloud backup, Trakey Web sync and account records

If you are signed in, Trakey can back up your data to your account automatically, approximately every three hours and when you leave the App. You can also use Back up now. A restore occurs only when you choose Restore or accept a restore offer after signing in.

A backup may include profile details such as name, email, bio and any location information you entered; notes, goals, habits, tasks, Learn and Challenge content and statistics; game statistics; settings; and your Gemini API key. It does not include your profile photo, music, recordings, temporary calculator, currency or focus history, or your App lock PIN and settings.

Backups are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account ID in compressed form. Google provides protections for data in transit and at rest. Trakey does not add a separate encryption layer of its own to these backups. Do not back up information you are not comfortable storing in a cloud database.

When signed in, your notes, goals, tasks, question folders and Learn cards may also sync with Trakey Web at maniaxe.in/trakey. Changes can be synchronised between the phone and web service.

When you back up, the App also creates an account entry in its administrative database. This may include your account ID, name, email, bio, location if supplied, join date, last active time and App version. We use this information to operate the service, provide support and understand usage. Access is restricted to authorised people responsible for operating Trakey. Rating and feedback status may also be associated with your account.

6. Trakey AI, translation, document scanning and voice

Trakey AI, translation, image translation, Learn scan and question-import recovery use Trakey's AI server, which runs on Cloudflare. When you use these features, the text, images, documents or other content you submit, together with the instructions needed to process it, may be sent to that server and forwarded to an AI provider. Groq handles most text requests, while Google Gemini may process photos, documents, scans and certain fallback requests.

These providers process submitted content under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit information you do not want processed by these services.

To identify requests and apply daily limits, the server checks a sign-in token. If you are not signed in, the App may create an anonymous guest account through Firebase Authentication without a name or email address. The server maintains daily request counters linked to the account ID and may use a short one-way network-address fingerprint for guest abuse prevention. Trakey's AI server is not designed to retain the content of AI requests or generated answers. Infrastructure and AI providers may process technical request data, including IP addresses, under their own policies.

You may add your own Gemini API key under Settings > Customise. When you use that option, requests are sent directly from your device to Google using your key rather than through Trakey's AI server. Your key is stored on your device and may be included in your cloud backup. Anyone with access to it may be able to use your quota, so keep it private.

Voice input uses the speech-recognition service available on your device or through Google. Text-to-speech uses your device's speech engine. The applicable provider's privacy practices govern its processing.

AI-generated content can be incorrect, incomplete or outdated. Please consult the Terms and Conditions.

7. Third-party services and advertising

Trakey connects to third-party services to provide specific features. Depending on the service and the request, those services may receive your IP address, device information and technical request data. The information processed depends on the feature you use, the service configuration and your privacy choices.

  • Google Firebase Authentication: sign-in, anonymous guest accounts and account identification.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore: cloud backup, account records, social features and synchronised content.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: announcements, update notes and push notifications. Google may generate an FCM token for your installation.
  • Google AdMob: displays optional rewarded advertisements. Watching a rewarded advertisement can unlock access to two designated features in Trakey. When an ad is requested, displayed or interacted with, AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions, interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals. Depending on configuration, consent choices, device settings and applicable services, information may also be used for advertising personalisation or measurement.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: host Trakey's AI server, apply usage limits, process request metadata and forward AI requests.
  • Groq: processes most Trakey AI text requests and certain translation, alert or task-related text requests.
  • Google Gemini API: processes photos, documents, scans, question imports and certain fallback AI requests.
  • Google speech services: provide speech recognition or related voice functionality where used.
  • Web3Forms: delivers feedback and support messages to us by email.
  • Microsoft Clarity: may be used to understand product usage and improve the experience where integrated and enabled.
  • Currency-rate providers: Frankfurter, a jsDelivr-hosted currency API and open.er-api.com provide public exchange rates for requested currency codes.
  • GitHub: the App checks release information to notify you of updates and downloads an update file if you choose to do so.
  • Google profile-photo servers: may provide your Google profile photo, which can be cached by the App.
  • External links: links to services such as Telegram, YouTube, Google Maps, Google AI Studio and our website open in a browser or another app and are subject to those services' own policies.

We do not intentionally send your private notes, goals, tasks, recordings or other private personal content to AdMob for advertising. AdMob's normal advertising operation may nevertheless process technical and advertising-related information as described above.

Google's processing of advertising data is governed by Google's applicable terms and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

We do not control independent third-party services and cannot guarantee their separate privacy practices. Please review their policies where appropriate.

8. Permissions

Permissions are requested for the features that need them. You can generally change permissions in Android settings, although disabling a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

  • Notifications: reminders, alerts, rate alerts and announcements.
  • Exact alarms: reminders scheduled for specific times.
  • Microphone: voice input, Translator voice mode and Recorder.
  • Camera and photos or files: image translation, Learn scan, profile-photo selection, saving images and importing questions or music.
  • Internet and network state: online features and the third-party services listed in this policy.
  • Biometrics: optional App lock authentication. Android handles fingerprint or face verification and tells the App whether authentication succeeded; the App does not receive your biometric template.

Trakey does not request contacts, SMS, call-log or precise-location permissions as described by the current App implementation. This statement should be reviewed if permissions change in a future release.

11. App lock and security

App lock is optional. If enabled, you create a PIN of four to six digits.

  • The App locks again after a period away from the App, according to the selected security settings.
  • The Extra secure option may hide the App preview in the recent-apps list and block screenshots or screen recording inside Trakey, depending on the Android implementation.
  • The PIN is stored as a salted, hashed value in the App's private device storage rather than as plain text. It is not uploaded or included in Trakey's cloud backup.
  • Fingerprint or face authentication is handled by Android.
  • After repeated incorrect PIN attempts, the App may impose a waiting period.
  • PIN recovery and reset depend on the App's implemented recovery options and your device security settings. Clearing App data can erase local information that has not been backed up.
  • Changing or disabling the PIN requires the current PIN or the supported recovery process.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including Android's app sandbox and encrypted connections to our services where supported. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and operating system updated and protected. Rooted or compromised devices may expose local information.
