1. About Trakey and this policy

Trakey is an Android productivity, learning and personal growth application developed by an individual developer using the Trakey Labs brand. Maniaxe.in provides website resources, hosting, support information and publication of release information and installation files for Trakey.

References to "Trakey", "Trakey Labs", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the Trakey application and its individual developer operating under the Trakey Labs brand, as appropriate to the context. Trakey Labs is a brand name and does not, by itself, represent a separately incorporated company.

This policy explains the information that may be stored, accessed, transmitted or processed when you use Trakey. Actual processing depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, the app version, the SDKs included in that version and the settings of third-party services.

2. Privacy at a glance

  • Many productivity features store information locally on your device.
  • Google sign-in is optional for ordinary local features but is required for certain account, backup, synchronization and social features.
  • Optional cloud features store selected information in Firebase.
  • Trakey offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Analytics and diagnostics services may process usage events, technical information and crash reports, depending on the enabled integrations.
  • AI and translation features may transmit content you submit to the relevant processing service and AI provider.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • You can manage certain information in the App and request help with deletion by contacting us.

3. Information stored locally

Depending on the features you use, Trakey may store the following information in the App's private storage or other device locations selected for a feature:

  • Profile information, including your name, bio, avatar choice and selected profile photo.
  • Notes, goals, tasks, habits, milestones, calendar entries, reminders and organisation data.
  • Focus sessions, activity statistics, game statistics and challenge results.
  • Learning cards, question folders, imported questions and learning history.
  • Calculator history, saved currency preferences, exchange-rate alerts, world clock cities and saved country information.
  • Translation history and saved translations.
  • Voice recordings created using the Recorder.
  • Images you choose to save from challenge results.
  • Music library information and playlists used by the music player.
  • App preferences, notification settings, theme and AI settings.
  • A Gemini API key if you choose to supply one.
  • App lock settings and a PIN verifier if App lock is enabled.

Locally stored content is not automatically uploaded merely because you create it. It may be transmitted if you use cloud backup, synchronization, AI processing, feedback, sharing or another feature that requires it. Files may also be accessed when you explicitly select them for import, processing or sharing.

4. Google sign-in and account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may process your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile-photo URL, as provided by the sign-in process. Trakey uses account information to identify your account, display your profile and provide account-dependent features. Trakey does not receive your Google password.

Some features may create an anonymous Firebase Authentication account when you are not signed in. Such an account may have a unique identifier without a name or email address. It can be used to identify requests and apply usage limits.

5. Cloud backups, synchronization and account records

If you sign in and use cloud backup, Trakey may upload selected information to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. Backups may be created automatically or when you select a backup action, depending on the App version and settings. Restoring a backup is initiated through the available restore controls.

A backup may include:

  • Profile information, such as name, email, bio and location information you entered, where supported.
  • Notes, goals, tasks, habits, learning content and challenge statistics.
  • Game statistics, settings and other supported application data.
  • A custom Gemini API key, if the version you use includes that key in the backup payload.

The exact contents depend on the version of Trakey. Review the information shown in the backup controls before using them.

When signed in, supported notes, goals, tasks, question folders and learning cards may synchronize with Trakey Web at maniaxe.in/trakey.

The administrative database may contain account identifiers, contact details, join dates, last-active information, app version, ratings or feedback status, where those fields are implemented. Access should be limited to authorised people who need the information to operate or support the service.

Firebase provides infrastructure protections, including encryption in transit and at rest for supported services. Trakey does not claim that every backup has additional end-to-end or application-level encryption. Do not back up information you are unwilling to store with a cloud provider.

6. AI, translation, scanning and voice features

When you use Trakey AI, translation, image translation, document scanning, learning scans or question-import recovery, the text, images, documents or other content you submit may be transmitted to the service required for that feature.

Trakey's AI service uses Cloudflare infrastructure to handle supported requests. Groq processes most text requests, while the Google Gemini API may process images, documents, scans or certain fallback requests. The specific provider depends on the feature and current implementation. These providers may process submitted content and associated technical request information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The service may use a Firebase account identifier to enforce daily usage limits. A short-lived or one-way network-address fingerprint may be used for guest abuse prevention if that mechanism is enabled. We do not claim that all infrastructure providers immediately erase all request metadata; their own retention rules may apply.

If you supply your own Gemini API key, supported requests may be sent directly from your device to Google using that key. The key is sensitive and may be included in cloud backup if the relevant backup implementation includes it. Protect the key and review the backup contents.

Voice recognition may use an Android or Google speech-recognition service. Text-to-speech may use your device's speech engine. Audio processing and retention depend on the selected implementation and provider.

AI-generated responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. Avoid submitting sensitive information unless you understand and accept the processing required by the feature.

7. Third-party services, analytics and advertising

Trakey uses third-party services to provide selected features. Depending on the service, its SDK configuration and your choices, those providers may receive technical information, device information, identifiers, network information or content required to provide the requested feature.

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Google sign-in, anonymous accounts and account identification.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore: cloud backups, account records, social features and supported synchronized content.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications and announcements. An installation may receive an FCM token.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: app usage events and related analytics data, where analytics collection is enabled.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports, diagnostic information, app version and related technical details, where enabled.
  • Google AdMob: optional rewarded advertisements. Ad requests, impressions and interactions may involve IP address, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, fraud-prevention signals and advertising measurement information. Personalised advertising, where available, depends on configuration, consent, device settings and applicable law.
  • Microsoft Clarity: product usage analysis and diagnostics where the relevant SDK is integrated and enabled. The information collected depends on the SDK configuration and the features supported by the mobile implementation.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: AI request handling, usage limits and request metadata where those services are used.
  • Groq: supported text-based AI and related requests.
  • Google Gemini API: supported image, document, scan, text and fallback AI requests.
  • Google speech services: speech recognition where used.
  • Web3Forms: delivery of feedback and support submissions.
  • Currency-rate providers: Frankfurter, open.er-api.com and a jsDelivr-hosted currency API, where used by the current version.
  • GitHub: release information and update downloads when the App uses the GitHub release mechanism.
  • Google profile-photo services: retrieval of a profile photo when you use Google sign-in.
  • External websites and applications: linked services such as YouTube, Telegram, Google Maps and Google AI Studio when you choose to open them.

The list above describes integrations reported for Trakey's features. It is not a guarantee that every service is enabled in every release. Only services actually included or used by the published version should be represented as active integrations.

Other analytics or advertising SDKs, if added in a future release, will be subject to the applicable disclosures and settings. Their identities and data practices should be added to this policy before they are enabled.

We do not intentionally send private notes, goals, tasks or recordings to AdMob for advertising. Nevertheless, AdMob and its partners may process technical and advertising-related information during normal ad operation.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, Microsoft Privacy Statement, Cloudflare Privacy Policy, Groq Privacy Policy.

8. Permissions and device access

Trakey requests device permissions for features that need them. Depending on the version and feature, these may include:

  • Notifications: reminders, alerts and announcements.
  • Exact alarms: reminders scheduled for specific times.
  • Microphone: voice input, voice translation and recording.
  • Camera: image translation, document scanning and other camera-dependent features.
  • Photos and files: profile-photo selection, importing questions or music, and saving images.
  • Internet and network state: online services and connectivity checks.
  • Biometrics: optional App lock authentication through Android's biometric system.

Android settings generally allow you to manage permissions. Disabling a permission may prevent the associated feature from working. The permissions actually requested by the published APK take precedence over this general description.

Trakey is not intended to collect contacts, SMS, call logs or precise location through dedicated permissions. Any future change in permissions or data collection must be reflected in this policy.

9. Notifications and announcements

If enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging may deliver announcements, app news, update notes and notifications to your installation. Google may generate and process an installation-specific messaging token for delivery. You can manage notifications in Trakey or Android settings where supported.

10. Feedback, ratings and support

If you submit feedback, a rating or a support request, Trakey may process the message and the details you provide, such as your name, email address, rating, date and time, app version, device model and Android version. Information may be sent through Web3Forms and may be associated with an account record when the implementation supports that association.

We use these details to respond, troubleshoot problems, maintain the service and improve the app. Do not include passwords, PINs, payment credentials or other authentication secrets in support messages.

11. App lock and security

App lock is optional. Where implemented, the PIN may be stored as a salted hash or other protected verifier in private device storage and should not be included in cloud backups. Biometric verification is handled by Android, which reports the authentication result to the App.

The App may use Android's application sandbox and encrypted network connections to protect information. Cloud providers apply their own security controls. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security. Rooted, compromised or shared devices may expose local information.

  • Signing out may remove signed-in information from the device without deleting a cloud backup.

The "Delete all data" control, where available, is intended to delete the categories described in its confirmation screen. Do not assume that it deletes every copy held by independent third-party providers, backups or previously submitted support messages.
