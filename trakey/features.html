Tips that are easy to miss

Many of Trakey’s best shortcuts are gestures. Tap and hold (long press) is the most useful one. Features can vary by version, region and plan.

  1. Hold a tool tile on Home
  2. Hold Quick 5 in Learn
  3. Hold the question card in Learn
  4. Hold the ✦ button to talk to Trakey AI
  5. Swipe the tools card on Home
  6. Hold to select in Organise
  7. Hold the weekly report card
  8. Scroll mode and recordings
  9. Share into Trakey from any app
  10. Add the Today widget
  11. Focus: keep the screen on or float the timer
  12. Tap the big cards
  13. My Chat: swipe, hold and Send to

1. Hold a tool tile on HomeHome

On Home, press and hold any tile in the tools card (Recorder, Music, Calculator, Translator, Timer, Currency, Scan, World clock, and the calculators on the second page). A small menu opens with two choices:

  • Quick access: puts the tool under Start focus on the Today card, so it is one tap away. You can keep up to four. You can also tap the faint + under Start focus to pick a tool from a list.
  • Home screen: asks your phone to add a shortcut icon for that tool to your phone’s home screen. Tapping it opens Trakey straight on that tool. Your phone’s launcher must support pinned shortcuts, and it may ask you to confirm.

To change a Quick access tool, hold it and choose another tool, or choose Remove. If a tool is already in Quick access, the hold menu shows Remove instead.

2. Hold Quick 5 in LearnLearn

On the Learn tab, the big Learned card has a Quick 5 button. Tap it for five quick questions with nothing to choose. Press and hold it to choose:

  • How many: 5 or 10 questions.
  • Where they come from: Trakey mix (a balanced mix, new cards first), All (any card, learned or not), What I’m learning (topics you have started), My cards (cards you added or scanned), or Choose topics (pick exactly which topics).

Your choice is remembered. When you change the number or the source, the button shows a small dot, for example Quick 10 •.

3. Hold the question card in LearnLearn

Under the big Learned card is a single question card. Tap it to show the answer, then tap the tick (“Got it”) to mark the card as learned. The next question takes its place.

To change which questions you get, press and hold the card and choose Questions from: Trakey mix, All (including your own cards), or any single topic. Your choice is remembered. The order is the same all day, and ticking a card moves you to the next one.

4. Hold the ✦ button to talk to Trakey AITrakey AI

Tap the sparkle (✦) button on Home to chat with Trakey AI. Hold it and speak to use your voice instead of typing. The first time, Android asks for microphone permission.

5. Swipe the tools card on HomeHome

Swipe the tools card on Home left to reach a second page of calculators: Percentage, Discount, GST (Tax outside India), EMI, Age, Bill split and BMI. Each one is its own tool and can also be held to add to Quick access or your home screen.

6. Hold to select in OrganiseOrganise

In Notes, Goals, Tasks, Calendar, Alerts and Habits, tap an item to open or edit it, and press and hold to select it. The header then shows delete and share. Deletes happen at once with an Undo bar, so a slip is easy to fix.

7. Hold the weekly report cardHome

The report card on Home (“Your week with Trakey”) can be changed by holding it: choose daily, weekly, monthly or don’t show again. You can also do this in Account > Report card, and tap its X to hide it for now.

8. Scroll mode and recordingsLearn · Recorder

  • In a Learn topic, switch to Scroll and swipe up for the next question and down for the previous one.
  • In the Recorder, tap a recording to expand and play it, and hold it for delete, share, favourite and rename.
  • On any bottom sheet, swipe down to close it.

9. Share into Trakey from any appScan

In your gallery, files app or browser, tap Share and choose Trakey. One or several photos or PDFs open in Scan, ready to become Learn cards, questions, text, a summary or a PDF.

10. Add the Today widgetWidget

Hold your phone’s home screen, choose Widgets, then Trakey. The widget shows what is coming up (alerts and tasks), opens Trakey when tapped and has a ✦ button for Trakey AI.

11. Focus: keep the screen on or float the timerFocus

In a Focus session, the sun icon keeps the screen on during the countdown. The floating-timer icon shrinks the timer into a small window (picture-in-picture) that stays on top while you use other apps.

12. Tap the big cardsHome · Learn

On Home, tap the big hero card to open Progress (EXP, stars, milestones and achievements). On Learn, tap the big Learned card for your Stats: the last 7 days, progress per subject and your weakest topics.

13. My Chat: swipe, hold and Send toOrganise · My Chat

In My Chat, swipe a message to the right to reply to it. Tap and hold a message to select it, then tap more to select others. With messages selected you can pin, edit, hide, delete or choose Send to, which offers only the places that fit, such as a Note, Task, Goal, Expense, List or Learn card, or Scan for a photo or PDF. Hold the My Chat tile in Organise to add it to Quick access or your phone home screen.

Home

  • Today card: your active goals and a Start focus button, with Quick access tools beneath it.
  • + Note, + Task, + List and + Alert: add something in one tap.
  • Tools card: Recorder, Music, Calculator, Translator, Timer, Currency, Scan and World clock, plus the calculators page.
  • Continue: one tap back to the last note, task list, Challenge or Learn topic you were in (forgotten after 5 days).
  • Recent notes, tasks and goals: tap one to open that exact item.
  • Report card: a weekly and monthly look at tasks, habit check-ins, goal steps and focus time, shareable as a PDF or saved as an image.
  • Game card, search, notifications, and your profile picture (opens Settings).

Organise

  • Goals with milestones, deadlines and progress.
  • Notes with folders, pinning, links that open on tap, numbered lists that continue, greeting cards, and share as PDF, image or WhatsApp. The note toolbar also has Calc and Translate.
  • Tasks: type a sentence such as “Pay rent tomorrow !high” and Trakey fills the date and priority. Tasks open in a details sheet. Steps inside a task and repeating tasks are Pro.
  • Habits with daily, weekly or monthly streaks.
  • Calendar with events and countdowns (repeating events are Pro).
  • Alerts: choose Alert (rings like an alarm) or Notify (notification only), with several ring sounds, vibrate or silent. Allow exact timing in Android settings so alerts fire on the minute.
  • Checklist for shopping and packing, and Expenses with monthly reports and CSV or PDF export.

My Chat

My Chat (in Organise) is a private space where you message yourself, like a notes-to-self chat. It is encrypted on your phone and is never sent to Trakey servers.

  • What you can add: text, photos, videos, PDFs and any other file (up to 100 MB each). Use the + button for Photos, Original, Files, Camera, a Note, a Recording or Scan.
  • Photo quality: Standard (default) saves space. Choose Original, or add a picture as a file, to keep it exactly as it is. You can change this in My Chat settings.
  • Reply, edit, pin and tags: swipe right to reply, hold a message to select it, then edit, pin or delete. Use #tags to find things later.
  • Formatting like WhatsApp: *bold*, _italic_, ~strike~ and `code`.
  • Search and filters: search messages, files and links, or filter by All, Pinned, Photos, Files and Links.
  • Send to: turn a message into a Note, Task (it reads “tomorrow” and “!high”), Goal, Expense (like “Lunch 120”), List (like “milk, eggs”), Learn card (like “question = answer”), a Translator entry or a Trakey AI prompt. Chat text only goes to Trakey AI if you choose to send it, and you press send yourself.
  • Save to My Chat: available in Notes, Scan, Trakey AI, Recorder, Calculators and Translator, so a result can be kept without leaving the tool. You can also share anything into it from another app: choose Share, then Trakey, then Save to My Chat.
  • Hidden messages: select a message and choose Hide from the three dots. It leaves the chat completely, including search, pins and tags. Open Hidden messages from the menu to bring it back to the same place (asks for fingerprint or face if App lock with biometrics is on).
  • Lock My Chat: if App lock is on, you can require your PIN or fingerprint every time it opens. While open, My Chat is hidden from screenshots and the recent apps view.
  • Backup: from the menu, choose Backup to keep an encrypted copy in your own Google Drive. It opens only with your backup password, which cannot be recovered, so keep it safe.
  • Export saves a zip (text only, or with media) in Downloads/Trakey. The zip is not encrypted. Saving a photo or file out of My Chat also creates a readable copy outside it.
  • Look: brightness, text size, wallpaper, message box colour and newest-on-top are in My Chat settings.

Learn

  • Cards and topics across subjects, shown as Cards, Scroll or List, with text-size controls and PDF sharing.
  • Add your own cards and notes to any topic, or scan pages into cards with AI.
  • Quick 5 and the question card (see tips 2 and 3), Continue, Search and Stats.
  • World: Countries and the World clock.

Challenges

  • Timed Practice and Exam runs from your own question folders or the built-in sets.
  • Questions: create folders, add questions and import them from TXT or PDF files that include the answers. Questions without an answer cannot be used.
  • History, analytics, a daily practice reminder (the bell in the header) and, on results, Plan revision, Save to Notes and Translate.
  • Spaced review and the Mistakes notebook are Pro.

Scan

Open Scan from Home > Tools or by sharing images or PDFs to Trakey. Choose what you want from your pages:

  • Learn cards or Questions
  • Extract text, on your phone (free, offline) or with AI, then edit, copy, translate or save as a Note
  • Summary: key points, a short summary or revision notes
  • Save as PDF: photos into one PDF, made on your phone with no AI and no limit

Other tools

  • Translator: text, voice, conversation and saved translations.
  • Calculator: a basic keypad, with scientific mode, unit converter and history in the top bar.
  • Currency: exchange rates and rate alerts. Timer: with a stopwatch in its top bar.
  • Recorder and Music player, Focus with calm sounds, and the Trakey Game brain games (Pattern Recall, Number Dash, Sequence Solver, Path Recall).
  • Tools are connected: the Calculator can send a result to Expenses or Notes, and the Translator can save to Notes.

Trakey AI

Chat, ask about photos and get help with planning, learning and writing. It works out of the box. Advanced users can add their own Gemini API key in Settings > Customise > Trakey AI. AI answers can be wrong, so check anything important.

Settings, account and safety

  • Account: profile, Google sign-in, Back up now, Restore, Report card, and Backup & data (Sync across devices, Auto backup, Google Drive, Clear local data, Delete all data).
  • Theme (Light, Dark or System), language, text size and notification choices.
  • Security: App lock with a 4 to 6 digit PIN, optional fingerprint or face unlock, and Extra secure (hides Trakey in recent apps and blocks screenshots).
  • Friends: choose a username, add friends and challenge them. Share my stats is off by default.
  • About: version, updates, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, rating and feedback.

Trakey Pro

Trakey Pro is optional. Free accounts have daily limits on Scan with AI, AI import and AI actions, a starter question library, and some locked features. See Free vs Pro in the App for the current list. Payment and ad details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms.
